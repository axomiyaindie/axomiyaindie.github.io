# Axomiya Indie

> **Building for Assam, for the Future.**

Hey, I'm from Assam. This is my personal corner of the internet, where I write down my thoughts, share what I'm building, and document the journey of an independent developer trying to make a difference.

## What is this?

This is my personal blog and project showcase. It's a space where I can be honest about my work, my ideas, and the things I care about.

- **Projects** — the things I'm building (PrepBuddy, AxomiyaGPT, and more to come)
- **Thoughts** — blog posts about learning, tech, culture, and life in Assam
- **About** — a place to learn more about me and connect

Everything here is available in **both English and Assamese**. Just click the language button in the top-right corner to switch. You can read everything in whichever language feels right to you.

## Why does this exist?

Two reasons.

First, I want to write. I have thoughts about technology, education, and what it means to build things from a place like Assam. This is where I put them down.

Second, I believe the internet should be for everyone, not just English speakers. There's no reason a student in Assam should have less access to information or ideas than someone anywhere else. By writing in both Assamese and English, I'm trying to close that gap, one post at a time.

## What can you expect?

- **Fast, clean, and distraction-free** — no ads, no trackers, no algorithms
- **Always bilingual** — every post exists in English and Assamese (or has a note explaining that the other version is coming)
- **Updated whenever I write** — the moment I publish something new, it appears here
- **Honest** — this is a personal blog, so you'll see my thoughts, not polished marketing
- **Open to help** — if you want to translate, report a typo, or just say hi, you're welcome to. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
