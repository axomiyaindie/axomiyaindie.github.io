# AxomiyaGPT: An AI That Speaks Our Language

> **Status: Coming soon.** AxomiyaGPT is in early development, at the pre-prototype stage. There is nothing to download or try yet. This page shares what we are building and why, so you can follow along from the very beginning.

---

## About AxomiyaGPT

AxomiyaGPT is an AI assistant that truly understands and speaks Assamese. It is not a tool that translates from English and hopes for the best. The goal is natural, culturally aware replies, written the way an Assamese speaker would actually say them.

Ask it a question in Assamese, and it should answer in Assamese that feels like it came from a person who grew up here.

---

## Why This Matters

Almost every popular AI assistant today is built mainly around English. Assamese is spoken by millions of people, yet to most AI tools it is a "low-resource" language, which simply means there is far less Assamese material for them to learn from.

The result is familiar to anyone who has tried:

- Awkward sentences that no one would say out loud
- Wrong words, wrong grammar, or a mix of Assamese and Bengali
- Answers that miss our festivals, food, places, and everyday context
- Names, places, and traditions that get twisted or ignored

When technology cannot speak your language properly, it quietly leaves you out. A student in a small town, a shopkeeper, a farmer, a grandparent who only reads Assamese: they should not have to switch to English to use the tools the rest of the world takes for granted.

AxomiyaGPT is our attempt to change that.

## What We Are Aiming For

These are the goals we are working toward. They describe the vision, not what is ready today.

### Natural Assamese conversation
Talk the way you normally talk. Formal or casual, short or detailed, the assistant should follow along without stiff, translated-sounding replies.

### Cultural context awareness
Bihu, gamosa, Majuli, tea gardens, local exams, local news, local ways of speaking. The assistant should understand what these mean to us, not treat them as foreign words.

### Voice in and voice out
Not everyone is comfortable typing in Assamese. We want you to be able to speak your question and hear the answer back, in Assamese.

### Works with low connectivity
Network access is not always reliable, especially outside the cities. We want AxomiyaGPT to be useful even when the internet is weak or unavailable.
