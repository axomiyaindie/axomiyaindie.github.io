## About AxomiyaGPT

AxomiyaGPT is an AI assistant that truly understands and speaks Assamese — not just translating from English, but generating natural, culturally-aware responses in our language.

### Why this matters

Most AI models treat Assamese as a low-resource language, producing awkward or incorrect output. AxomiyaGPT is built to change that.

### Features

- Natural Assamese conversation
- Cultural context awareness
- Voice input/output in Assamese
- Works offline for low-connectivity areas
