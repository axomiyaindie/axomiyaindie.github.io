# Introducing PrepBuddy: Every Assam Exam Aspirant Deserves One Good Place to Study

Imagine a student we'll call Bhaskar. He's 23, from a small town in Assam, and he has one dream: wearing the Assam Police SI uniform.

His mornings start with a YouTube lecture. By afternoon he's digging through a Telegram group for the right PDF. In the evening he's hunting for practice questions on a website full of ads, and at night he scrolls a WhatsApp group hoping to spot the next job notification before the deadline passes.

Bhaskar isn't lazy. He studies for hours every day. But he spends a painful chunk of those hours just *finding things*.

Thousands of aspirants across Assam live this exact routine. We built PrepBuddy for them.

---

## What if everything lived in one place?

PrepBuddy brings your whole preparation together: your syllabus, practice questions, mock tests, video courses, and job alerts. It's all in one app and one website, and it's made with Assam's exams in mind.

Here's what that looks like in real life.

### 1. Start with your exam, not with confusion

Pick your exam, whether that's Assam Police SI, ADRE Grade III, or APSC CCE. PrepBuddy shows you the full syllabus, neatly split into subjects and topics. You always know what to study next and how much is left.

### 2. Practise like it matters

Under every topic you'll find previous-year questions and fresh ones, and every answer comes with a clear explanation. You don't just find out *what* is right. You understand *why*. That's the difference between memorising and actually learning.

### 3. Face the exam before the exam

Mock tests are where confidence is built, so we made them feel real. Practise at your own pace, race a timer, or sit a full-length test with negative marking, just like the real thing. When you finish, you see your score, your rank, and a breakdown showing exactly where you're strong and where to focus.

### 4. Learn from courses and skill paths

Prefer to learn by watching? Join video courses taught by instructors. Want to build a real-world skill? Follow a step-by-step path and finish with a certificate you can be proud of.

### 5. Never miss a job opportunity again

This one is close to our hearts. PrepBuddy collects verified government jobs, private jobs, internships, and apprenticeships in one list. Then it does something simple but powerful: it reminds you **3 days, 2 days, 1 day, and on the last day** before an application closes.

No more finding out about a vacancy a day too late.

## Built for teachers too

Great teachers shouldn't need a big company behind them to reach students. On PrepBuddy, approved instructors can publish their own courses right from the same app. Earnings are tracked automatically, and payments go straight into the instructor's bank account every month.

If you've ever wanted to teach what you know, your students are already here.

## Your safety comes first

We know you're trusting us with your time, your money, and your personal details. So:

- Every file uploaded to PrepBuddy is checked for viruses first.
- Your password is strongly protected, and if you change it, you can be signed out of every device at once.
- Student accounts and staff accounts are kept completely separate.
- Payments are built so an internet hiccup never charges you twice.
