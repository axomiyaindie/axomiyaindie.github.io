## Why I built this

I wanted a personal site that:
- Lists all my projects in one place
- Shows real published/updated dates without manual typing
- Works in both English and Assamese
- Deploys automatically whenever I push

## The stack

- **Astro** for static site generation
- **GitHub Actions** for CI/CD
- **Git log** to auto-detect when content was added or updated
- **Content Collections** for project and blog data
